How to use ECHO

  • Top / New / Ask / Show / Jobs — pick what to read.
  • 🔥 Hot right now — sorts by how fast replies are arriving this minute (not total score).
  • Velocity meter — how quickly people are commenting. Full bar = busy debate.
  • ✨ Summarize the debate — AI explains what commenters are arguing about.
  • ☆ Save / ★ My rooms — keep stories you like, grouped your way.
