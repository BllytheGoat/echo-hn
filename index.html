How to use ECHO

  • Top / New / Ask / Show / Jobs — pick what to read.
  • 🔥 Hot right now — sorts by how fast replies arrive this minute.
  • Velocity meter — how quickly people are commenting.
  • ✨ Summarize — AI distills what commenters argue about.
  • 💡 Explain — plain-English context for newcomers.
  • 🔍 Source — paywall/archived/bias check on the link.
  • 👥 Who's talking — most-cited voices in the thread.
  • ⏱ Time travel — scrub back to earlier front pages.
  • 🔔 Watch — get a badge when keywords appear.
  • ★ Rooms — save searches & stories your way.
  • 📤 Share — turn a story into a card.
